# Seven Fingers

**Genre:** Gilbert & Sullivan-style patter song (original lyrics)
**Key / meter / tempo:** F major, 2/4, quarter = 144 (patter couplets in sixteenths, *più mosso*)
**Meter of text:** strict trochaic tetrameter (8 syllables, stress on odd syllables = on the beat);
patter couplets are trochaic octameter (16 syllables in sixteenths).

**Form:** Intro (2 bars) | Verse 1 (8 lines) + Patter 1 | Refrain | Verse 2 (8 lines) + Patter 2 | Refrain + Tag

### Self-critique and revision
- **Scansion:** every verse/refrain line counted to 8 syllables, patter lines to 16; natural word stress
  lands on odd syllables, which the score places on beats (eighths) or on each eighth-note pulse (sixteenths).
  "Every" is sung as two syllables (EV-ry) throughout.
- **Weakest spot in the draft:** V1 lines 3–4 were "Every knuckle bending sideways / Like a kelp bed in the
  tideways": a forced non-word rhyme and no joke. **Revised** to "bent and twisted / Six of which were never
  listed!", which scans cleanly and lands a punchline (the extra fingers are undocumented features).
- **Funny?** The comic engine is the professor's escalating spin against a list of horrors; the patter couplets
  end on a deflating turn (graph goes up / hand's a cupboard; marvel / marble), and the refrain lists failures
  as compliance.
