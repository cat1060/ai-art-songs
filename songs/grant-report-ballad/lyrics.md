# The Grant Report Ballad

**Genre:** soulful gospel ballad, 6/8, dotted-quarter = 54. Eb major; last chorus jumps up a whole step to F major via a C7 pivot.

**Form:** Intro (2 bars) - Verse 1 - Chorus - Verse 2 - Chorus - Bridge - Key change - Final Chorus (in F) - Tag

---

### Verse 1
```
Ebmaj7          Cm7               Abmaj7
Three years a - go in Sec - tion C
              Bb7               Ebmaj7    Bb7sus4
I promised human-level art
Gm7              C7
Now my model writes a sonnet
Fm7       Abm             Bb7   Bb7sus4
that rhymes "heart" with "heart"   (same note both times)
```

### Chorus
```
Abmaj7              Bb7
Oh, Broader Impacts, hold me close
Gm7                     Cm7
Intellectual Merit, pray (blue-note Gb melisma) for me
Fm7            Bb7
I'll call it "promising results"
Gm7        C7      Fm7    Bb7     Eb
and beg extension number three
```
