# Temperature 0.7

**Genre:** 1970s Barry White-style R&B love song. F minor, 4/4, about 74 bpm. Lush strings, a Rhodes pad, and a slinky bass riff (root, 5th, b7, octave).

**Form:** Spoken Intro, Verse 1, Chorus, Verse 2, Chorus, Bridge (spoken and sung), Chorus with an outro vamp

---

## Spoken Intro *(over a sustained Fm9 string pad, half-whispered)*

> Close the laptop lid halfway, baby. Leave the fans running.

> But you, baby... you're *calibrated.*
> Let me turn down the lights. And turn up the... entropy.
