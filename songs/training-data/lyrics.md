# It's Not Plagiarism, It's Training Data

**Genre:** Late-20th-century Broadway ensemble number (a "meeting" song in the patter-and-vamp tradition). Bb major, 4/4, "Brightly, with a soft-shoe vamp" (quarter = 132). Modulates up a half step to B major for the final chorus.

**Cycle position:** Song 3 of 5.

## Characters

- **DEAN HARGROVE**: wants this over by 4:00. Speaks in budget lines. Baritone.
- **DR. TYLER BRISK**: assistant professor, AI booster, has a startup and a vest. Tenor.
- **PROF. MIRIAM ACKROYD**: English and Comparative Literature, cross-listed against her will. Mezzo; dry as vellum.
- **CHAIR LOWENTHAL**: department chair, owns the gavel and has lost control of it. Bass-baritone.
- **ENSEMBLE**: the rest of the faculty, the minute-taker, and one emeritus who wakes up only on cue.
